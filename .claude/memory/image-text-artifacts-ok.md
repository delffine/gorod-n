Small garbled inscriptions and fake "artist signatures" that FLUX draws on the Bender-style illustrations (shop signs, plates, corner signature) are fine; the user likes them as an "artist's autograph" and an extra riddle.

But on 2026-10-09 the user flagged a published image (post 20261009_1404-proezd) with a whole English "poem" across the top and Bender with 4 ears — those are defects.

**Why:** Small marks look like real old illustrations; a block of text or extra body parts looks broken and embarrassing in a published post.
**How to apply:** Don't regenerate for small marks. Do regenerate (up to 2×) for text larger than a sign, extra/missing body parts, distorted faces, missing Bender. Count ears/hands/fingers when viewing. Keep proper names (Ostap Bender, Twelve Chairs, Kukryniksy) out of FLUX prompts — they get rendered as text.
